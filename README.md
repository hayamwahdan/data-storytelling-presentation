## 📌 Project Overview

A professional **10-slide presentation** exploring the concept of Data Storytelling — how businesses, like individuals, use data to reflect, decide, and communicate meaningful insights. Presented as part of a soft skills and communication training program, February 2026.

> *"Facts Tell, Stories Sell."*

---

## 🎯 Core Message

Data storytelling is not just about charts and numbers — it is about connecting data to human decisions. This presentation bridges personal life narratives with business decision-making, showing that the same sense-making process we use in life is what drives great business intelligence.

## 💡 Key Concepts Covered

**The Analytics Maturity Ladder** — a framework showing how organizations evolve from basic reporting to strategic decision-making across four levels: Descriptive ("What happened?"), Diagnostic ("Why did it happen?"), Predictive ("What will happen?"), and Prescriptive ("How can we make it happen?").

**The Human Story Cycle** — a model connecting personal life experience to business data processes, showing that reflection and decision-making are universal patterns whether in life or in business.

**The Data Storytelling Formula** — Data + Insight + Action + Audience — a practical framework for communicating findings that drive real business results.
